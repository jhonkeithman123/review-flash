# About ReviewFlash & The Creator

---

## ⚡ What is ReviewFlash?

**ReviewFlash** is an AI-accelerated active recall flashcard, spaced repetition, and adaptive assessment platform designed for ambitious learners, software engineers, medical students, and knowledge workers. 

Traditional flashcard applications are often static, tedious, and disconnected from the deep focus state required for mastery. ReviewFlash was engineered from the ground up to merge **evidence-based cognitive neuroscience** with **cutting-edge web engineering, tactile 3D interactions, and ambient focus environments**.

## 🧠 Core Pillars & Design Philosophy

### 1. **Evidence-Based Active Recall (SM-2 Spaced Retrieval)**
ReviewFlash implements the SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm to calculate optimal review intervals based on easiness factor ($EF$), repetition streaks, and recall latency. Cards you struggle with resurface frequently, while mastered concepts are spaced outward to lock into long-term memory.

### 2. **DITroy AI Ecosystem & Adaptive Intelligence**
Integrated with the **DITroy AI Engine**, ReviewFlash dynamically enriches multiple-choice quizzes with smart, logically plausible distractors, analyzes weak concept clusters, and provides contextual study clues without giving away the direct answer.

### 3. **Tactile 3D Motion & Fluid Micro-Interactions**
Every interaction in ReviewFlash is designed to provide immediate physical feedback:
* 3D card flips with perspective lighting.
* Physical card deal transitions, emerald mastery elevations, and rose review slides.
* Horizontal length morph animations when toggling between Grid and List view modes.
* Origin-anchored spring blossoming modals and cascading menus.

### 4. **Study Music Lounge (Ambient Deep Focus)**
Built-in YouTube Audio Engine streaming curated Lo-Fi, Synthwave, Classical, and Nature soundscapes directly in the browser with floating background playback and batch playlist importing.

### 5. **Data Sovereignty & Deck Forking**
Inspired by open-source collaboration, users can share decks with granular access controls or fork existing libraries into decoupled, sovereign clones with full ownership rights.
